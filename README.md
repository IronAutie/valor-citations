# Valor Citations

Documented acts of valor by Andrew Jonathan Fine.

## 1. The Window (Age 17)

A college sophomore at 17, sent home from chemistry class by Fr. Bush because his mother was worried. By the time he got home, his mother had barricaded the house and his father was trying to force entry through the door. He tried a window instead. Andrew stood on the inside, took a karate stance, and gave him his best Gandalf. His father smashed through the windows, used the phone, and left rather than beat his mother.

## 2. The Wrists

On a visit home, Andrew stopped his mother from slashing her wrists.

## 3. Carol

Andrew stopped his first wife Carol from a planned murder-suicide by pills overdose, the night before she was to preach as a student pastor. He saved her life and her student pastorate.
